Understanding the Multi-Station Analysis

This report uses real-time data from multiple Environment Canada hydrometric stations across Colchester County to assess regional drought conditions and apply appropriate stress testing to well risk calculations.

Monitoring Stations:
  • Salmon River near Truro (01EO001): Central Colchester region
  • Economy River near Economy (01EB001): North coast region
  • Great Village River at Great Village (01ED002): North-central region
Drought Stress Levels:
  • SEVERE: 67%+ stations critical → 4.0m additional drawdown applied
  • MODERATE: 33-66% stations critical → 3.0m additional drawdown applied
  • NORMAL: <33% stations critical → 2.0m standard drawdown applied

Understanding the Columns

  • WELL_ID 📊: Click the chart icon to view a detailed, easy-to-understand report for that specific well
  • buffer_m (Buffer in Meters): Vertical distance between stressed water level and pump depth. Negative values indicate critical risk.
  • drying_risk: Risk category based on buffer after drought stress testing
  • stressed_water_level_m: Depth to water after applying regional drought drawdown
  • drought_drawdown_m: Additional drawdown applied based on current surface water conditions
  • current_water_level_m_observed: Original depth to water before drought stress
  • pump_depth_m: Estimated pump depth (80% of well depth or 2.5m from bottom)
  • DEPTH: Total drilled depth of the well
  • YIELD: Well flow rate in L/min

Note: This analysis provides a regional approach to drought risk assessment, giving more accurate results than single-station analysis for county-wide groundwater conditions.
